Integrated-System GP Models
===========================

``MOLGP`` assembles system and reaction observations for the original mapped
evaluator interface.  ``MOLGP2`` supports the libxc-baseline kernels used for
separate full-XC components.  Both classes fit a sparse control-point GP and
map its kernels to serializable inference objects.
